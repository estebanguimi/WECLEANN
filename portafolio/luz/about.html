About

Design, camera
and cut.

I started in 2018 as a graphic designer in Peru, making logos, ads and weekly content for Soporte Total GUIMI, Arturo's Burger and Digitalia TEC. That is where I learned to work to fixed deadlines and to design pieces that sell.

From 2022 to 2024 I worked freelance: matchday posters for Universitario, Sport Huancayo and the Peru women's national team, social coverage of the Qatar 2022 World Cup, content for Physo, the Zena Coffee online store and streaming overlays.

Today I am based in Miami. At Harris Media Group I edit, animate and shoot video for U.S. campaigns (Sam Gallucci, NH Senate Republicans, Bobby Charles), and I also photograph concerts for MercyMe, Crowder and for KING & COUNTRY.

Available for full-time and freelance work
